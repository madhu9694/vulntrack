Report Contents & Standard Sections

VulnTrack utilizes ReportLab to build audit-ready PDF reports including:

  • Cover Page: Title, target URL, tester, date, and confidentiality disclaimer.
  • Executive Summary: High-level business overview and metric breakdown table.
  • Methodology: OWASP WSTG framework, ZAP and Burp Suite tools, Top 10:2025 taxonomy.
  • Explainable Risk Formula: Mathematical 40/25/25/10 breakdown for transparency.
  • Technical Findings: Affected endpoints, HTTP methods, parameters, and evidence.
  • Remediation Status: Clear audit trail of verified fixes vs persisting vulnerabilities.
  • Page Numbering: Two-pass numbered canvas ("Page X of Y").
PDF files are generated dynamically on demand directly into an in-memory byte buffer without saving temporary clutter to disk.
